Fly your unicorn horn through a neon rainbow-colored, dodge the walls, and collect notes to add layers to the music.

![](https://github.com/leereilly/prism-drift/raw/main/assets/gameplay.webp)

## Controls

- **Start:** Space or Enter
- **Steer:** Arrow keys or WASD
- **Slow motion:** Hold Space while flying
- **Pause/resume:** Esc or P
- **Restart:** R
- **Invert axes:** Pause, then toggle left/right or up/down inversion
